The Grimace Lab

There is a rigid grimace standing between who you are and who you are becoming. Let's dismantle it together.
Origin

The foundation of the Lab emerged during ethnographic research on the contemporary use of makeup among young women in Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso. It was there that the idea of makeup evolved into a deeper conceptual lens.

In Ouaga, makeup went beyond the gendered use of cosmetics; it was an active social grimace. Serving an explicit function of social and self-navigation, these are the somatic habits we forge to stay alive. A social grimace designates any form of habitual bodily contortion triggered by specific interactions with others or one's own internalised thoughts.

Soon, it became clear that this practice was not only cultural; it was also deeply human. While fluid bodily contortions and movements allow us to communicate, rigid grimaces systematically freeze parts of the body, paralysing our creative impetus. These rigid bodily contortions do not appear out of nowhere; they are the somatic architecture of beliefs taught by one’s families, schools, and institutions.

Your tight neck and frozen jaws are an internalised rulebook of movements—a muscular tension arising as an immediate reflex to a learned emotional trigger.

Unfortunately, some of the habits learned from these institutions are harmful to our bodies. The contemporary labour market requires this numbness to function. It enforces artificial rhythms that force your biology to match an institutional timeline, flattening creative depth into mere performance.

The Strategy of Interception

Grimace Consciousness rejects the pattern of escaping to a separate meditation cushion just to tolerate a toxic baseline. True wakefulness demands unselective feeling and careful interpretation at your desk, mapping physical contractions directly to inherited institutional metrics.

The 4-Week Laboratory Syllabus
"I am doing this because I believe in the liberatory power of ideas. The grimace lab utilises socio-anthropological and philosophical concepts to help set individuals and institutions free from their rigid grimaces. During my PhD, I came across concepts that can experientially shift your organs around. Not only did these concepts shift my relationship with my body and mind, I found them to be great burnout prevention tools." — Dr. Fanidh Sanogo, Founder

We do not treat academic texts or somatic guidelines as static data to be memorised, but as visceral scalpels. If an idea cannot help you map or dismantle the exact institutional metrics currently dictating your micro actions, it does not belong in this Lab.

Week 1

Anti-Oedipus & Control

Mapping how modern achievement frameworks capture the nervous system, turning the jaw and neck into an internalised regulatory machine. Initiating the daily Grimace Audit loop.

— G. Deleuze & F. Guattari
Week 2

For Opacity

An absolute corporeal refusal to make your internal terrain transparently legible, manageable, or extractable by corporate or academic performance metrics. Cultivating somatic boundaries.

— Édouard Glissant
Week 3

Creative Evolution

Halting the "snowball" of institutional stress. Learning to notice the unceasing internal variations of your mental state before they accumulate enough mass to force a rigid, defensive attitude onto your body.

— Henri Bergson
Week 4

The Invention of Women

Decoupling the body from the visual dominance of meaning creation. Undoing the physical policing anchored into our daily micro-movements to cement a permanent baseline of sovereign rest.

— Oyèrónkẹ́ Oyěwùmí
The Golden Rule
The Core Rule: To "Rest wide awake," one must never skip a breath. As soon as you skip a breath, you must move in another direction—mentally and physically.
Sit up. Drop your eyebrows. Relax your jaw. Let your head float on an aligned neck. Relax your stomach. Know that you are safe, even if you die. Access this state, and execute entirely from here.

To heal from automated habits, you must allow those identities created through rigid grimaces to die. This is not passive relaxation, it is a visceral rejection of what was forced down your throat without your consent. A body unable to creatively evolve is a body open to manipulation.
